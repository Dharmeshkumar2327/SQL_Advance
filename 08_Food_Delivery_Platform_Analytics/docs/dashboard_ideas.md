# Dashboard Ideas

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
- KPI cards: Total Orders, Completed Orders, Net Revenue, AOV, Cancellation Rate, On-Time Delivery Rate.
- Monthly revenue trend and order volume trend.
- Revenue by city and cuisine.

## Page 2 — Restaurant Performance
- Restaurant revenue ranking.
- Top-selling menu items.
- Average restaurant rating.
- Completion and cancellation rate by restaurant.

## Page 3 — Customer Intelligence
- RFM segment distribution.
- Repeat customer percentage.
- Customer lifetime value ranking.
- Monthly cohort retention heatmap.

## Page 4 — Delivery Operations
- Average delivery minutes by city.
- On-time delivery percentage by partner.
- Late delivery distribution.
- Delivery ratings.

## Page 5 — Payments & Discounts
- Payment method mix.
- Successful / failed / refunded payments.
- Discounts by month.
- Discount-to-revenue ratio.

Suggested Power BI model relationships should follow the primary/foreign keys in the SQL schema. Prefer importing curated views for the first dashboard version.
